> **A fast price checker for Moonlighter and Moonlighter 2.**

I made this for myself and for anyone who wants to spend less time guessing prices and more time playing.

Most of what you sell comes from the items and relics you gather while exploring dungeons, so the guide is built around one simple idea: **find an item, check the best price, sell it, move on.**

## What it does

- **Search** item and relic prices instantly
- **Switch** between Moonlighter and Moonlighter 2
- **Track** items you've already checked
- **Import / export** your progress
- **Filter** by dungeon, region and rarity
- **Estimate Moonlighter 2 boosts** from quality, popularity, furniture and shift perks

## Why

No giant spreadsheet. No digging through long guides.

**Open → search → price → back to the game.**
